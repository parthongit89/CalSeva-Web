Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 28, 2026

CalSEVA ("us", "we", or "our") operates the CalSEVA web application and technical calibration services (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal and operational data when you interact with our Service.

1. Information Collection & Use

Our core privacy philosophy is simple: Your data belongs to you.

  • Data Protection: All equipment details, machinery specifications, QR codes, scheduling information, and inquiry data submitted by clients are handled with strict confidentiality.
  • No Unsolicited Tracking: We do not collect, transmit, or sync your private industrial calibration logs to remote third-party databases without your consent.

2. Permissions & Inquiry Forms

To provide full calibration functionality and customer support, CalSEVA may process information submitted via:

  • Service Request Forms & QR Codes: Contact information (Name, Email, Phone, Company) and equipment parameters are used solely for fulfilling calibration service inquiries and scheduling.
  • Document & Report Exports: Exported PDF reports, calibration certificates, and QR code graphics are generated directly for client access.

3. Third-Party Services

The application may utilize standard web standards and basic performance analytics to maintain site security and uptime. No personally identifiable information (PII) or confidential industrial asset details are ever sold or shared with external advertisers.

4. Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS / TLS 1.3) and secure edge routing via Vercel to protect all data transmissions. We recommend that clients maintain secure passwords and device lock settings to safeguard exported calibration certificates.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
