Our Calibration Services

Temperature Controller Calibration

Verification and tuning of control outputs (PID, relay, analog) to ensure precise thermal regulation and process stability.

Temperature Indicator with Sensor Calibration

System-level calibration pairing indicators with their dedicated probes to eliminate combined display and sensing errors.

RTD Sensor Calibration

Resistance-versus-temperature testing (Pt100, Pt1000) using high-precision baths and dry blocks to verify curve compliance and linearity.

Thermocouple Sensor Testing

Millivolt output verification and junction integrity testing across operating ranges to detect signal drift, degradation, or wiring faults.

Loop Calibration

End-to-end testing of the complete signal chain—from sensor input through transmitters to the PLC/DCS—ensuring seamless overall loop accuracy.

AMC Services (Annual Maintenance Contracts)

Scheduled preventive calibration cycles, priority on-site callouts, and ongoing certificate management to maintain continuous audit compliance.

On-site calibration is a calibration service performed at the customer's facility, allowing applicable measurement equipment to be calibrated without being transported to another location.

On-site calibration can help reduce equipment transportation, minimize downtime, and make the calibration process more convenient for businesses.

The equipment and measurement parameters supported depend on the specific calibration requirement. Contact our team with your equipment details so we can assess the requirement.

You can contact CalSEVA through our enquiry form, phone, or email. Share your equipment and calibration requirements, and our team will assist you with the next steps.
